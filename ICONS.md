Icon sources and licenses
=========================

- `packs/simple-icons/icons/*.svg` — Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org).
  The project releases its set under CC0 1.0, but individual icons may carry
  their own license; every shipped icon's upstream `license` metadata was
  audited. Non-CC0 icons (Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, MIT, Unlicense,
  CC-BY-*, CC-BY-SA-*, CC-BY-NC-*, GPL, vendor trademark policies) are
  attributed per-icon in `packs/simple-icons/NOTICE`, and each file carries
  an SPDX-License-Identifier comment. Most SVGs were modified to inherit
  `fill="currentColor"` so GoRex can tint them — modified copies of
  share-alike, copyleft and NC-SA icons stay under the same license.
  `tauri.svg` (CC-BY-NC-ND-4.0) ships verbatim because its license forbids
  derivatives. Icons marked `custom` carry vendor brand guidelines and are
  used nominatively, to identify the program the icon depicts.
- `packs/lobe-icons/icons/*.svg` — LobeHub Lobe Icons
  (https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), MIT License.
  `packs/lobe-icons/NOTICE` contains the license text.

Brand icons are trademarks of their respective owners and are used here
only to identify the programs they depict.
